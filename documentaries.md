# Documentaries

Documentaries of the [World of Adventures](https://www.youtube.com/@mirprikliuchenii) studio (Мир Приключений) on Orthodox monasteries and shrines, grouped by place: on Mount Athos, each monastery and skete has its own section. Most films are the English version of the series History and Sanctuaries of Mount Athos; the others are Russian originals with English subtitles. 23 films, all on YouTube.
