# Awesome Orthodoxy [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Websites, texts, music, media, and software of the Eastern Orthodox Church.

The [Eastern Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church) is a communion of self-governing churches that share the faith, worship, and tradition of the early Christian Church. This list covers English-language and international resources. Lists in other languages follow the same structure and are linked below.

Contributions are welcome. Read the [contribution guidelines](contributing.md) first.

## Official Websites

- [Ecumenical Patriarchate](https://ec-patr.org) - First see of the Orthodox Church, based in Constantinople.
- [Patriarchate of Alexandria](https://www.patriarchateofalexandria.com) - Church of Alexandria and all Africa.
- [Patriarchate of Antioch](https://www.antiochpatriarchate.org) - Church of Antioch and all the East, based in Damascus.
- [Patriarchate of Jerusalem](https://en.jerusalem-patriarchate.info) - Church of Jerusalem and guardian of the holy places.
- [Orthodox Church in America](https://www.oca.org) - Autocephalous church in North America.
- [Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of America](https://www.goarch.org) - Archdiocese of the Ecumenical Patriarchate in the United States.
- [Antiochian Orthodox Christian Archdiocese](https://www.antiochian.org) - Archdiocese of the Patriarchate of Antioch in North America.
- [Russian Orthodox Church Outside of Russia](https://www.synod.com) - Self-governing part of the Russian Orthodox Church for the diaspora.
- [Archdiocese of Thyateira and Great Britain](https://www.thyateira.org.uk) - Archdiocese of the Ecumenical Patriarchate in the United Kingdom.
- [Assembly of Canonical Orthodox Bishops](https://www.assemblyofbishops.org) - Body of all canonical Orthodox bishops in the United States.

## News and Media

- [Orthodox Times](https://orthodoxtimes.com) - Daily news from the Orthodox world.
- [OrthoChristian.com](https://orthochristian.com) - English edition of Pravoslavie.ru, with news, articles, and lives of saints.
- [Orthodox Christian Network](https://myocn.com) - Media ministry with articles, podcasts, and videos.

## Scripture and Liturgy

### Holy Scripture

- [Septuagint at Elpenor](https://www.ellopos.net/elpenor/greek-texts/septuagint/default.asp) - Greek Old Testament with a parallel English translation.
- [OCA Scripture Readings](https://www.oca.org/readings) - Daily readings according to the Orthodox lectionary.

### Liturgical Texts

- [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org) - Texts and Byzantine chant scores for the daily services, in Greek and English.

### Calendars

- [Holy Trinity Orthodox Calendar](https://www.holytrinityorthodox.com/calendar/) - Daily calendar with saints, readings, and troparia.

### Lives of Saints

- [OCA Lives of the Saints](https://www.oca.org/saints/lives) - Lives of the saints commemorated each day.

## Books and Libraries

- [OrthodoxWiki](https://orthodoxwiki.org) - Free encyclopedia of Orthodox Christianity.
- [Early Church Fathers](https://www.ccel.org/fathers) - Public domain translations of the Church Fathers from the Christian Classics Ethereal Library.
- [St Vladimir's Seminary Press](https://www.svspress.com) - Publisher of Orthodox theology, patristics, and liturgy.
- [Holy Trinity Publications](https://www.holytrinitypublications.com) - Publishing house of Holy Trinity Monastery in Jordanville.
- [Ancient Faith Store](https://store.ancientfaith.com) - Books, icons, and recordings from Ancient Faith Publishing.

## Articles and Blogs

- [Public Orthodoxy](https://publicorthodoxy.org) - Essays on Orthodoxy and public life from the Orthodox Christian Studies Center of Fordham University.
- [Pemptousia](https://pemptousia.com) - Online journal on theology and Orthodox culture, linked to Vatopedi Monastery on Mount Athos.

## Audio

### Podcasts

- [Ancient Faith Podcasts](https://www.ancientfaith.com/podcasts) - Network of Orthodox podcasts on scripture, theology, and parish life.
- [The Lord of Spirits](https://www.ancientfaith.com/podcasts/lordofspirits) - Podcast on the Orthodox understanding of the spiritual world.

### Radio Stations

- [Ancient Faith Radio](https://www.ancientfaith.com/radio) - Internet radio with Orthodox music and talk programs.

## Music

- [Cappella Romana](https://cappellaromana.org) - Vocal ensemble performing Byzantine chant and Slavic choral music.

### Sheet Music

- [OCA Music Downloads](https://www.oca.org/liturgics/music-downloads) - Free scores in English for the services of the Church.

## Iconography and Sacred Art

- [Byzantine and Christian Museum](https://www.byzantinemuseum.gr/en/) - Museum in Athens with collections of icons and Byzantine art.

## Apps and Software

### Desktop Software

- [Neanes](https://github.com/neanes/neanes) - Open-source scorewriter for Byzantine chant notation.

### Fonts

- [Church Slavonic Fonts](https://sci.ponomar.net/fonts.html) - Unicode fonts for Church Slavonic from the Slavonic Computing Initiative.

### APIs and Data

- [Orthocal API](https://orthocal.info/api/) - Free JSON API with daily readings, commemorations, and fasting rules.
- [orthocal-python](https://github.com/brianglass/orthocal-python) - Source code of the Orthocal calendar service.

- [Orthodox Christian Fellowship](https://www.ocf.net) - Ministry for Orthodox college students in North America.
- [Orthodox Christian Mission Center](https://www.ocmc.org) - Missionary agency of the Orthodox Church in the United States.
